一封来自济明团队的信

A Letter From the ZenCare Team

亲爱的朋友，您好：
Dear Friend,

见字如面。

常有朋友问我，「济明」二字有何深意？其实，"济"是渡人以善，"明"是察病于微。这不仅是一个名字，更是我们心底对传统中医智慧的敬意，以及对你健康的挂念。

在快节奏的马来西亚，我们发现许多身体的反覆发炎与不适，根源往往离不开脾胃与内在气血的失衡。因此，济明始终坚持「不只治病，更治人」。我们不追求短暂压制表面的疼痛，而是希望从整体出发，帮你把身体的"根本"照顾好，唤醒生命原有的自愈能力。

一转眼，我们已陪伴并守护了超过一万名患者。每一次的望闻问切，每一张处方的斟酌，都是我们对"医者仁心"最纯粹的坚守。

我们深知，推开诊所大门的那一刻，你交托的是无比珍贵的信任。为了这份信任，我们在此向你承诺：这里的诊疗收费永远公开透明，绝无隐形消费；我们坚持使用100%无类固醇、无西药添加的天然草药；并严格落实环境消毒，坚持使用一次性针具与床单，只为给你最安全、干净的调理环境。

不管未来的日子有多忙碌，济明中医都会始终在这里，以专业与温暖，做你最安心的健康后盾。

祝 喜乐，安康。

It feels good to write to you, as if we were speaking face to face.

Friends often ask what "ZenCare" truly means. "Zen" is to help others with kindness; "Care" is to see illness clearly, even in its earliest signs. It is not just a name — it reflects our deep respect for the wisdom of traditional Chinese medicine, and our genuine concern for your health.

In fast-paced Malaysia, we've seen that recurring inflammation and discomfort often trace back to an imbalance in digestion and internal qi and blood. That's why ZenCare has always believed in "treating the person, not just the illness." Rather than simply masking surface symptoms, we take a holistic approach — caring for the root cause and awakening your body's own capacity to heal.

In what feels like the blink of an eye, we've walked alongside and cared for over ten thousand patients. Every diagnosis, every prescription we consider carefully — each one is our purest commitment to compassionate medicine.

We know that the moment you walk through our doors, you place an invaluable trust in us. In return, we promise you this: our fees are always transparent, with no hidden charges; we use 100% natural herbal medicine, free of steroids or western drug additives; and we strictly enforce sterilization, using single-use needles and sheets, to give you the safest and cleanest environment for your care.

No matter how busy life gets, ZenCare TCM will always be here — with professionalism and warmth, as the health partner you can rely on.

With joy and good health,

济明中医 全体团队 敬上
With Sincere Regards,
The ZenCare TCM Team

Why Choose ZenCare

01

辨证论治 · 个性化方案

Personalized TCM Diagnosis

坚持望闻问切四诊合参，从整体出发找出病根，根据每位患者的体质与生活习惯，量身定制最合适的调理方案。

Using the four diagnostic methods — observation, listening, inquiry and pulse-taking — we identify the root cause holistically and tailor a treatment plan to each patient's constitution and lifestyle.

专业医师团队

Professional Practitioner Team

医师毕业于中国中医院校，同时拥有马来西亚卫生部行医执照，拥有十余年临床经验，兼具传统底蕴与现代视野。

Our practitioners are graduates of accredited TCM institutions in China and hold practicing licenses from Malaysia's Ministry of Health, bringing over a decade of clinical experience with both traditional depth and a modern perspective.

We strictly control herb quality, using 100% natural herbal medicine with no steroids or western drug additives. All fees are transparent, with absolutely no hidden charges.

Single-use sheets and needles for every session, with strict sterilization protocols — a safe, comfortable and clean clinical environment.
