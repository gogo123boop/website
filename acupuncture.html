📍

针灸治疗

Acupuncture Therapy

以无菌毫针精准刺入穴位，调节经络气血、缓解神经筋膜压力，达到疏通经络、调和阴阳的治疗效果。

Sterile fine needles are precisely inserted at acupoints to regulate meridian qi and blood, relieving nerve and fascial tension.

🦴

整骨治疗

Bone-Setting Therapy

运用手法调整骨骼与关节位置，恢复身体结构平衡，改善因错位引起的疼痛与功能障碍。

Manual techniques adjust bone and joint position, restoring structural balance and relieving pain caused by misalignment.

🔄

关节松动治疗

Joint Mobilization

透过温和的关节松动手法，改善关节活动度、减轻僵硬与疼痛，恢复关节正常功能与灵活性。

Gentle joint mobilization techniques improve range of motion, reduce stiffness and pain, and restore normal joint function.

⚡

神经脱敏治疗

Nerve Desensitization

针对敏感或受压的神经进行手法与针法治疗，降低神经敏感度，缓解神经性疼痛与异常感觉。

Manual and needling techniques target sensitive or compressed nerves, relieving neuropathic pain and abnormal sensations.

🫙

拔罐放血治疗

Cupping & Bloodletting

结合拔罐与放血疗法，排除局部瘀滞、改善血液循环，有效缓解肌肉紧张与深层组织炎症。

Combining cupping and bloodletting therapy to clear local stagnation, improve circulation, and relieve muscle tension.

⚖️

结构功能治疗

Structural Function Therapy

评估整体身体结构与功能关系，透过针对性手法与训练，恢复身体力学平衡与动作协调性。

We assess overall body structure and function, using targeted techniques and training to restore mechanical balance and coordination.

🩹

包扎治疗

Bandaging & Taping

运用弹性绷带与贴扎技术，固定保护受伤部位、减轻肿胀，提供关节与肌肉稳定支撑。

Elastic bandages and taping techniques stabilize and protect injured areas, reduce swelling, and support joints and muscles.

📋

运动康复评估

Rehabilitation Assessment

根据个人肌力、生活型态与疼痛部位，量身设计运动训练处方，强化核心肌群、预防再发。

Based on individual strength, lifestyle and pain location, we design a personalized exercise program to strengthen core muscles and prevent recurrence.
